Title: The Last Beat

[Scene 1 – Mika's street, dusk]
NARRATOR: On the corner of Maple and Ninth, everyone knows the rent is due on the first, and Mr. Grimm owns the first, the second, and every day after that.

MIKA (sad, trembling): An eviction notice... Grandma Rosa has lived here for forty years. They can't sell our building.
[Visual: Mika clutching her sketchbook, low angle, rain starting]
[SFX: rain, distant thunder]
[Music: melancholic]

[Scene 2 – The chase]
NARRATOR: Two hundred signatures. That was the number. And Mika had exactly one afternoon.
MIKA (determined): Run, Tommy! Every door, every floor, GO!
TOMMY (panicked): Mika, wait for me! My legs are tiny!
NARRATOR: They raced through the alleys, up the stairwells, past every door on the block.
[SFX: footsteps, whoosh, heartbeat]
[Music: tense]

<Song: Rooftop Anthem – hip hop, trap, street, young male voice – leads: jayden, zoe>

ZOE (rap): They came for the building, came for the view. But the heart of this city isn't concrete, it's me and you. Sign your name, stand in line, watch the whole block shine. One mic, one beat, one roof at a time.
RICO: Everybody hands up! This block is OURS!
