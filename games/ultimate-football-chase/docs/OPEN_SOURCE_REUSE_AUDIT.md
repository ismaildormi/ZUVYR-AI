## Why a simple recolour is not enough

Recolouring a clone while retaining the same models, artwork, animations, music, branding or near-identical presentation may still infringe rights or violate store copycat rules. MIT/Apache rights apply to *licensed material* only; adverts, SDKs and in-app purchases also need independent ownership, configuration, privacy compliance and device testing. An MIT shader/script does not provide a finished, licensable mobile game.

## Safe implementation boundary

Keep Unity as previously selected. Use original characters, kits, teams, music and scenes; adopt only demonstrably licensed third-party **components that improve the design**, with preserved notices and separate asset provenance. Do not mix entire incompatible projects or replace the PDF's exact gameplay figures. The source project is still greybox and **not compiled/tested in Unity**; a production-quality mobile title requires original/cleared 3D art, animations, rendering, builds and device QA. The separately written browser sketch is not the product or an acceptable visual target.
