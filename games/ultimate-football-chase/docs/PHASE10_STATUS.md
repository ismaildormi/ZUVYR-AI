# Phase 10 — local mission loop, seasonal route and guest-data controls

**Historical snapshot:** [V2 playable browser companion and expanded roster/market](V2_PLAYTEST_STATUS.md) came later.

**Source complete, Unity acceptance pending.** This phase builds on phases 1–9 in the isolated Unity project; it does not enable real-money services or authoritative contests.

## What changed

- **Mission chain and HUD:** An original, explicitly *local preview* mission group needs 15 actual accepted jumps, 10 successfully avoided fictional slide tackles and 100 collected coins (the PDF's examples). Completing a group advances the display multiplier from x1 up to its PDF cap of **x30**. Group order/repetition and the sample local-score formula `(floor(distance metres) + 10 × collected coins) × multiplier` are **provisional**, not a final ranked formula. Counters persist in the existing guest/UID-scoped **non-authoritative Hub preview** on completed runs; they are shape-sanitized on load. The run HUD shows the current group's numeric progress, multiplier, explicitly unverified local score and personal local-preview best (highlighted near the previous best). Account-cache merges select one newer snapshot, not sum events from different devices. No multiplier applies to demo coins, prizes or leaderboard points. Buffered jumps count when the runner actually starts jumping; invulnerable/board-protected tackle contacts do not count as dodges. Rewards from signed-in runs remain unavailable.
- **Seasonal runner route:** Four original road/lane/banner palettes use the same two-or-three-month UTC season cycle as the Hub. They change only decoration outside the lanes, **not** obstacle geometry, spawn plans, scores or player safety. Future event assets can be added without altering the obstacle planner. No exclusive seasonal characters/items, remote live events or purchase offers.
- **Wardrobe:** Every one of the four original greybox silhouettes has five per-character provisional colour treatments. Teal/amber/violet are free; Storm unlocks after five **preview stages**, Dawn after ten. These are not final outfit meshes, server-granted ownership, paid cosmetics or spendable entitlements. The sanitizer rejects an outfit whose preview stage is too low.
- **Privacy:** The Hub privacy panel has an explicit two-step **reset of this device's guest progress, local settings, mission preview and demo wallet**. It is disabled during account restoration and for signed-in users. It clears guest-claim markers but never deletes UID-scoped account caches or claims cloud-account deletion. When a signed-in account lacks a verified/cached Hub profile, a direct runner-scene launch now blocks unverified run recording and offers a way back to the Hub.
- **Tests added:** `Phase10Tests.cs` checks mission completion/restore/cap, score preview, no mid-run account snapshot replacement, kit gates/sanitization, and matching obstacle geometry between seasonal palettes. These tests are **authored, not executed**; Unity Editor is not installed here.

## Remaining acceptance and missing product work

1. **Unity/device verification:** Import in Unity 6.3, generate scenes, compile and run the Editor Test Runner, run Android/iOS device/long-run QA, check touch/safe-area, score/mission event counts, Arabic font/RTL, audio focus/mixing and frame rate. Static parsing is not a substitute.
2. **Authority/security:** Build and configure a trusted backend. Validate gameplay events/server time; derive scores server-side; reject implausible runs; verify monthly top three and prize recipients, independently close/reset/archive rankings, select any top-50 random award, fulfil prizes. The current client score, mission counters, stages and demo balances are all **untrusted and cannot source standings, wallets, contest results or prizes**.
3. **Live economy and competitions:** Real Showdown matching/entry/outcomes and exclusive unlocks; paid/free season pass and seasonal campaigns; authenticated wallet/inventory/receipts; gems, keys, boxes and paid cosmetics; real ad SDK and callback-verified rewards; limited daily offers; store billing and restoration. All remain off until their services, review and consent policies exist.
4. **Production content:** Distinct rigged character bodies, animation/style, proper 3–5 **outfit models** per character, eight original league kit/logo sets, accessories, boards, final scenery/lighting/particles and produced sound/music; interactive story/boss and fully authored seasonal event content. Current concept colours, text and procedural audio are greybox only.
5. **Accounts/privacy/localization:** Configure/test Firebase, identity providers, rules and cloud sync; implement verified account deletion, consent withdrawal and real purchase restoration; write privacy policy. Translate all remaining English UI/content to Arabic/French/English (a further small set of runner HUD and daily-mission strings is now authored) and integrate proven Arabic fonts/RTL layout. The region control remains a preference, not eligibility. Verify iOS Ambient mixing and Android nonexclusive audio with other phone music on real devices.
6. **Separate repository:** GitHub `createRepository` permission was denied previously. The owner instructed not to retry; the isolated game remains in this branch until the owner resolves repository permissions.

**No winners, standings, spendable currency, real prizes or events are fabricated.** Example Firestore rules are not deployed. Firebase/ads/IAP SDKs and device signing materials are not included. All unpublished values are labelled provisional.
