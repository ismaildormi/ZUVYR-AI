Title: The Last Token

[Scene 1 – Arcade, night]
NARRATOR: After midnight, the arcade on Ninth Street belonged to one boy, and one boy only.
RICO (excited): Zoe! Zoe! I found it behind the claw machine. The golden token. The legend is REAL!
ZOE (sarcastic): Rico, that thing costs fifty cents. I have three in my pocket.
[SFX: sparkle]
[Music: hype]

[Scene 2 – Back room]
ARCADE OWNER: Kids, that token opens the old prize vault. Nobody has won it in twenty years.
RICO (nervous): Twenty years? What happens if I lose it?
ARCADE OWNER: Then Mr. Grimm buys this place by Friday.
[Visual: dusty vault door, single hanging bulb]

[Scene 3 – The vault]
NARRATOR: Rico slid the token into the slot. The vault groaned, the lights burst alive, and the whole arcade held its breath.
RICO (determined): Come on, come on... YES! Jackpot! Zoe, we saved the arcade!
ZOE (joyful): One spin, one win. Okay, legend. Okay.
[SFX: whoosh, crowd cheer]
[Music: hopeful]
