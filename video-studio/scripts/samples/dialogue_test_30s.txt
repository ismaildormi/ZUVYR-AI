Title: The Notice

[Scene 1 – Apartment hallway, evening]
MR_GRIMM (greedy, cold): Such a charming little home. (chuckle) Sign the paper, and I'll let you keep the memories for free.
TOMMY (fearful, trembling): Miss Mika... his smile is not smiling. Are we losing our home?
MIKA (sad, breaking): I don't know, Tommy. My hand won't stop shaking. This hallway is the last thing that smells like home.
MR_GRIMM (sigh): Sentiment. The most expensive hobby of the poor.
[SFX: door slam]
